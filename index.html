What We Do

Screen Work for Your Pool Cage & Lanai

From a couple of torn panels to a full pool-enclosure re-screen, we bring the screen, the tools and the crew — and leave your porch tight, clean and bug-free.

Pool Screen Enclosures

Full pool-cage and lanai re-screens — every old, sagging panel pulled and replaced with fresh, tight screen so you get your whole enclosure back.

Panel & Lanai Re-Screening

Ripped, stretched or storm-torn panels swapped out with the rolling-spline tool for a clean, drum-tight finish that blends right into the rest of the cage.

Aluminum Bars & Rebuilds

Bent, missing or added-on aluminum framing — we reinforce the structure and add new bars wherever your enclosure needs them before the screen goes back on.

Screen Doors & Pet Doors

New and reinforced screen-door closures, plus people-doors and doggie-doors installed or swapped — so the whole porch opens, closes and seals the way it should.
